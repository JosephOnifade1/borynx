# Borynx buyer-quality and optional paid services

**Charge for help hiring or extra visibility, not permission to hire.** Normal posting, pitching, awarding and kickoff are free. The two paid offers are separate, off-by-default manual pilots. The app never creates an invoice, collects money, sends campaign email, or moves funds between marketplace participants.

## Quality and reputation are not for sale

- Public launch requires the existing [verified email sign-in](./MARKETPLACE_PHASE_3.md). Development personas can still rehearse the regular lead workflow. On new submissions the buyer describes a clear deliverable, gives a positive budget and unit, and enters a delivery timeline (separate from the optional bid deadline). A site reviewer must publish the brief. Paying for a service never bypasses this review.
- One active lead per buyer account: a pending or returned brief and a published, unawarded open lead each occupy the slot. Awarding, closing, withdrawing, rejection, or removal frees it; a bid deadline by itself does not close it. Existing leads are not removed or backfilled. The same submission key still returns the original lead; direct and email-intake submissions share the same transaction lock and limit.
- New awards and recorded completions grant no karma. Previous ledger history is unchanged. Reviews still belong to individual completed awards, but only each buyer account's most recent eligible rating contributes to the average. The average appears at three distinct buyer accounts and drops away below three. Individual eligible testimonials remain visible. Report a fabricated or incentivized review for site-wide review; existing holds and moderation apply. Testimonials do not verify work, payment, or people's identity. The [staff queue](./MARKETPLACE_PHASE_6.md) can show advisory patterns but never acts on them automatically.

## Operate a small manual pilot

1. Recruit 5–10 buyer accounts with a real brief and an expected budget. Start with one category in which Borynx can supply qualified applicants. Once a buyer's open lead is manually published, its owner can see opt-in **Hiring services** below the brief. They explicitly consent to contact at their confirmed email when they request an offer. Guests, other buyers, and demo sessions cannot use paid requests.
2. **Assisted Hire** (`BORYNX_ASSISTED_HIRE=1`): the pilot price is **₦25,000 per brief**. Agree the scope and delivery date before invoicing. Commit to 2–3 available, budget-fit freelancers within two business days of confirmed payment. The in-app target counts two weekdays on the Lagos calendar; arrange any holiday exception before collecting payment. Confirm candidates' consent before making introductions. Refund the service fee if the agreed shortlist is not delivered. This is not a guarantee of hire.
3. **Sponsored lead** (separately set `BORYNX_SPONSORED_LEADS=1` and `BORYNX_SPONSORED_LEAD_PRICE_NGN` to an approved positive whole-naira fee): **do not enable until there is enough traffic to sell useful placement**. Check availability before invoicing. A verified administrator who records received payment starts a seven-day reservation. At most one slot can be reserved at once. On `/l`, an eligible placement is separately labeled **Sponsored lead** and must match the current board filters. It does not replace or reorder organic results or add a verification badge. Awarded, closed, expired, removed or space-restricted listings stop appearing immediately. A reservation ends at expiry or recorded refund, even if the slot stopped being visible earlier. Refund the placement fee if Borynx cannot deliver the agreed placement for a lead that remains eligible. If the lead stops accepting pitches, display ends without an automatic refund. There is no guarantee of hire.
4. A confirmed-email site administrator opens **Moderation → Pilot operations** or `/me/lead-services`. Agree terms and use an approved, separate invoicing and refund system. Only record confirmation **after** verifying the payment there, and record delivery or refund only **after** it has happened. The buyer sees their own status on the same page; other buyers and non-admin moderators cannot see pilot contacts or orders. An administrator cannot handle their own request. Keep formal receipts in the approved system of record, not in marketplace notes. If stopping the pilot, turn the flags off to remove offers and display while still letting operators finish or refund existing orders.

## Verification and rollout

```sh
npm run test:db -- --buyer-pilot-only
npm run test:db -- --lead-reviews-only
```

The pilot suite uses a disposable database and fictional verified sessions, with email sends disabled. It checks simultaneous direct submissions, consent and publishing boundaries, admin-only transitions, a single sponsored slot under contention, organic feed stability, and opt-out. The full database suite also races direct submission against a verified intake using a simulated email provider. Migration `0014_lead_quality_and_buyer_pilot` adds a nullable timeline and the app-only `borynx_private.lead_service_orders` table. The table has RLS and no browser-role table or sequence grants. It stores the quoted whole-naira fee in kobo and a reference to the consented sign-in identity, not bank details or a copy of the email. Lead/buyer deletion cascades the app record; removing the identity clears its contact reference.

No flags should be enabled against shared or hosted data without approving the exact verified sign-in, migration and operations setup first. No changes to local Neon/demo sign-in settings are required. Track paid briefs per qualified offer, on-time shortlists, buyer response to qualified pitches, refunds and contribution after payment and labour costs. As a directional gate, look for at least 3 of 10 qualified buyers paying and positive contribution. Consider hosted checkout with verified, idempotent webhooks only after the manual service works. Keep worker payments, escrow, extra-listing fees and generic ads out of this pilot.
